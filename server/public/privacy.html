Leafkin Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 October 2026

Leafkin identifies plants from photos and helps you care for them. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and who it is shared with. Leafkin has no user accounts, and we never ask for your name, email or location.

Operator: [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY], [COUNTRY]. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]

1. Data that stays on your phone

  • Your scan history, saved plants ("My Plants"), watering schedule, reminder time and language choice are stored only on your device.
  • Watering reminders are scheduled by your phone itself; no reminder data is sent to us.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

2. Photos you scan

  • When you scan a plant, the photo (resized to at most 1024 pixels) is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards it to Google's Gemini API to produce the identification, care, health and edibility information.
  • Our server does not store your photos. It only keeps a count of how many scans were made.
  • We use Google's paid Gemini API service. Under its terms, Google does not use the photos or results to improve its products, and keeps them only briefly for abuse monitoring. See Google's Gemini API terms.
  • Please don't photograph people or personal documents — only plants are needed.

3. Anonymous device ID

The app creates a random ID when installed. It is sent with each scan so the server can apply the daily scan limit. It is not linked to your identity, is kept by the server only for the current day, and is reset if you reinstall the app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • Leafkin is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about ad interactions to show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before ads are personalized; you can change your choice at any time. On iPhone, ads are only personalized if you allow tracking.
  • You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Crash reports (Sentry)

If the app crashes or hits an error, a report is sent to Sentry, our error-monitoring provider. It contains technical details only: device model, operating system, app version and what went wrong in the code. It does not include your photos or plant data.

6. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Scanning photos and applying the daily scan limit: to provide the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Crash reports: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Personalized ads: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); non-personalized ads: our legitimate interest in funding a free app.

7. Your rights

You can request access to, correction or deletion of personal data, object to processing, or withdraw consent at any time by contacting us at the address above. Because we don't hold accounts or store your photos, most data can be removed simply by uninstalling the app. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

Leafkin is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA), and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
